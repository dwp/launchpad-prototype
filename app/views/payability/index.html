What it is

This prototype explores how agents can capture and manage accommodation stays that may affect a claimant’s PIP payments. By structuring the experience around discrete records, clear statuses, and flexible data capture, the prototype supports agents in navigating stay histories, tracking incomplete information, and maintaining accurate records.
